Been Together

App Redesign - Reconnect with Users

View Final

What is Been Together?

Been Together is a well-known app used to track anniversaries for couples. It has +10M downloads on Google and Apps Store.

 

Its target users are couples, 16-30 years old, seeking an app to keep track anniversaries and important days.

Project Background

The Problem

Been Together DON'T have REAL connection with users

  • The app is known for being one of the biggest player in the couple app market, lacking a connection with users creates a big gap in their branding

Users feel demotivated to use the app

  • Users feel nothing with the app
  • The app does not support users' relationships

My Role

For this first personal project, I gave myself a chance to try everything, including:

  • UX research
  • Strategic design
  • UI design
  • UX Writing

The Challenge

Redesign an app that provides strong connection to users

Research

Understanding what users want and need from Been Together

I started out by conducting user interviews, competitive analysis and reading feedbacks from Google and App Store. My goals were to learn:

  • What are users' expectations, frustrations and emotional states when creating registries?
  • What service does the competition offer?

Key insights: Users find the app simple yet lacking

Initially, users are excited to set up their Been Together app.

  • Creating anniversary reminders are a staple and mark to show that they are in an official relationship.

 

But, they quickly forget about the app

  • They said the app were simple to use but had nothing to keep them engage to visit often.

 

Competitors offer the same service and not much have connection options

  • Doing so would help Been Together gain a competitive advantage and stand out from the crowd.

User journey map: from excited to demotivated

From the desk research and interviews, I started to create the current user journey map to identify pain points:

  • Users quickly feel demotivated to check the app frequently
  • Notifications are either helpful or overwhelming, leading some users to turn them off.
  • The app doesn't help users feel more emotionally connected to their partners.
  • Users don't know all the app features.

Strategize

Solution - increase app visit and emotional connection

  • Introducing new shared partner features - album, notes, and diaries
  • Creating an onboarding tutorial to guide users through the features
  • Removing redundant features and adding personalized notifications.

⇒ These will keep the simplicity of the app while increase personalization and connection.

Test

Two issues found in the test

I gave the medium-fidelity design to previous interviewees to ask for feedback and usability testing. There are two problems with it:

  • Data backup is complicated
  • There is mixed feedback with the color choice

Solutions

  • Add sign in/sign up ⇒ no need for data backup
  • Page customization

Reflection

To validate the content, I'd ask...

Since this was my first time conducting testing, I did not do that well and missed out on some important notions. If had the chance, I'd improve the effectiveness by asking:

  • What would you expect from this feature?
  • How would you like to be in contact with your partner? E.g., text notifications?
  • What's the most important thing you want from the app?

To optimize, I'd create a soft launch and users feedback loop

  • I'd gather early feedback and address potential issues before a full-scale launch.
  • I'd collect real-time feedback through in-app surveys or reviews to iterate quickly and improve the app experience.
