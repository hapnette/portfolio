What is Frank?

Frank is a common app for university students in Finland with lots of discount and student card. Its users' main goals are to:

  • Show student status through Frank card
  • Find discount quickly
  • Redeem and use discount codes efficiently

Project Background

The Context

This is my final project for the User Experience Design course of Aalto Strategic Experience Design summer program.

The Challenge

Build a seamless flow for student discount browsing and status verification, enabling effortless saving and easy access for users.

Research

Interviewing fellow students

As the app is designed for student, I took advantage and did some interview with my classmate. After that, I realize user's goals are:

  • Show student status through Frank card
  • Find discount quickly
  • Redeem and use discount codes efficiently

Crafting an user persona

One thing I found useful after the course is to design for human. Design should have a story-telling aspect with human actively involved in the process. In order to do so, an user persona would help designers visualize user's motivation and pain points.

Test

To validate the wireframe, we had a testing session where each student was able to conduct at least 3 user testings. During the test, I asked users to perform an overall flow and some specific task.

Key Issues

  • Discount cards have too many buttons, and it's not obvious that the cards themselves are clickable.
  • Favorites need a clearer hierarchy so saved discounts are easier to find and prioritized.
  • The student card should include a clear "Valid" preview to make verification more straightforward.

Refinement

  • Simplifing the discount card.
  • Placing Favorite tab into a more prioritized position.
  • Creating a visible valid status to strengthen user confidence.
