What is Mikaeli?

Mikaeli is a stunning concert and congress hall in Mikkeli. It is a symbol of this small town in Finland. Its website serves as a way for users to buy online tickets, book venue, and order meals.

Its target users are local residents, event attendees, organizers, and tourists looking for clear information about Mikaeli and its offerings.

My goal was to make the digital experience as smooth and welcoming as the on-site one.

Project Background

The Context

The original Mikaeli website was hard to navigate and overwhelming.

  • Information was scattered across long text-heavy pages
  • Key tasks like booking tickets, reserving intermission snacks, or finding contact info were difficult to complete

It lacked English language support, limiting access for international visitors.

Users felt confused or gave up halfway.

  • Visitors couldn't quickly find event or venue info
  • Organizers lacked clear guidance on how to host their events
  • Content and navigation were not tailored to different user needs

The Challenge

Redesign a website that is logical, accessible, and easy to use for all audiences.

Audit

Before starting to do anything, I looked into the web page first to understand the flow, their offerings, and purpose. I crafted the current web flow and noted things that feel off or non-logic flow. I also did some competitor/market research (theaters and venues in finland and in the world)

Research

Understanding what users want and need from Mikaeli

I interviewed 3 users - 1 complete new user, 1 frequent user, and 1 non-frequent user.

Key insights:

  • No english option
  • Confusing UI writing
  • Have all information but not with a logic flow.
